The Process

Your Path to College Soccer

Three structured steps designed to get the right players in front of the right coaches — nothing more, nothing less.

01

Register & Prepare

Select your age division and regional camp. Submit your player profile — club, position, GPA, graduation year. College coaches review rosters in advance and identify players they want to watch.

02

Compete & Be Evaluated

Three days of structured training, small-sided games, and full matches — designed to surface tactical intelligence, technical ability, and athletic profile. Every player is scored on a standardized rubric.

03

Get Identified

Participating coaches receive your evaluation report and contact information. Players flagged for interest receive direct outreach within 14 days. No middlemen. No club politics.

Who We Are

The Standard for
Soccer ID Evaluation

Elite Showcase ID was founded on a single premise: the best young soccer players in America deserve a direct path to the coaches who can change their futures — without the noise of politics, club affiliations, or pay-to-play systems getting in the way.

Our camps are designed by coaches, evaluated by coaches, and trusted by coaches. Every format decision — from the rubric to the schedule — exists to surface genuine talent, not to reward players who already have exposure.

Register Your Player

Real College Coaches

We vet every attendee. Every coach at our events is an active staff member from a college program — no scouts, no agents, no intermediaries.

Standardized Evaluation

Every player is scored on the same rubric. No favoritism, no first impressions. Just data coaches can trust and players can learn from.

Direct Pathways

Players flagged for interest receive direct coach outreach within 14 days. No middlemen, no recruitment agencies, no additional fees beyond registration.

All Clubs Welcome

Registration is first-come, first-served. Your club affiliation, rank, or region does not affect your ability to register or be evaluated fairly.
